Research

Our agents are built by people who publish. This is peer-reviewed work in computer vision and explainable AI by our CTO and academic co-authors, published by Springer Nature in 2026.

Discover Artificial Intelligence, volume 6, article 278, 24 February 2026

YOLO-driven computer vision framework for paddy disease

Santosh Kumar, Sumit Kumar, Durgesh Nandan

A real-time object-detection framework for spotting crop disease in field images. The same detection approach underpins Xentovia Vision AI: finding and counting objects reliably in messy, real-world camera footage.

Read the paper (doi 10.1007/s44163-026-00945-6)

International Journal of Computational Intelligence Systems, 22 October 2026

ExplainableCropAI: Interpretable deep learning for agricultural disease diagnosis and decision support

Santosh Kumar, Sumit Kumar, Durgesh Nandan

A deep-learning diagnosis system that shows which parts of an image drove each decision. The same principle runs through our products: every extracted field links to where it was read, and every lead score explains itself.

Read the paper (doi 10.1007/s44196-026-01654-9)

Both papers are open access under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Santosh Kumar and Dr. Sumit Kumar are affiliated with Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Pune; Dr. Durgesh Nandan with SR University, Warangal.
