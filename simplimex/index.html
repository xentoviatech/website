The Common Bottlenecks

Why Manual Documentation Slows You Down.

Traditional customs filing relies on hours of manual typing and constant cross-checking across different forms.

2 to 4 Hours Spent Per Shipment

Customs staff spend hours re-typing numbers from commercial invoices, certificates, and packing lists into eSANCHIT & ICEGATE.

3 to 7 Day Customs Holds

A single typo in an HS code, unit price, or consignee address triggers customs queries, leading to costly shipment delays.

Overflowing Documentation

Teams get overwhelmed managing thousands of email attachments, scanned invoices, and shipping bills every month.
