Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Xentovia Tech Private Limited ("Xentovia," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who visit our websites, use the Xentovia platform — our AI agents for voice, chat, WhatsApp, documents and vision, and the products built on it — or otherwise interact with our services (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information. By accessing or using our Services, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and job title when you create an account or join our waitlist.
  • Payment Information: Billing address and payment details processed securely through third-party payment processors. We do not store full payment card numbers.
  • Workflow & Content Data: Workflow configurations, prompts, API keys (encrypted), and data you process through the Services.
  • Communications: Messages you send to us via email, support tickets, or feedback forms.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, workflow execution logs, API call metadata, timestamps, and session duration.
  • Device & Browser Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and screen resolution.
  • Cookies & Tracking: We use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, and analyze usage patterns.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Data from authentication providers (e.g., Google, GitHub) when you use single sign-on.
  • Information from integrated third-party services you connect to your workflows.
  • Business contact information from publicly available sources or partners.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our Services.
  • Process transactions and send related information (confirmations, invoices).
  • Personalize and improve the user experience of our Services.
  • Analyze usage patterns to develop new features and optimize platform performance.
  • Send administrative communications (security alerts, updates, support messages).
  • Send marketing communications where you have opted in (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted vendors who assist us in operating our Services (cloud hosting, payment processing, analytics, customer support), subject to confidentiality obligations.
  • AI Model Providers: When you use an AI feature, the content needed for that request is sent through our own model gateway to the third-party AI model or speech provider that runs it (e.g., Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Sarvam AI). We encourage you to review their privacy policies.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize the sharing of your information.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • TLS encryption for data in transit.
  • Each organisation's data isolated at the database level, not only in the application.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC).
  • Encrypted storage of API keys, tokens and credentials.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our Services. We also retain data as necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Workflow execution data and logs are retained for up to 90 days unless you configure a different retention period.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in our emails.
  • Cookie Preferences: Manage cookie settings through your browser or our cookie consent tool.
  • Data Processing Objection: Object to or restrict certain processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@xentovia.ai.

7. International Data Transfers

Our Services are hosted in India (Mumbai). Some AI model and speech providers named in section 3 process requests outside India. Where data leaves India, we rely on those providers' data processing terms and send only the content a request needs.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (under 18, as defined by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at privacy@xentovia.ai, and we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Third-Party Links & Integrations

Our Services may contain links to third-party websites and integrate with third-party services. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you connect to through our platform.

9.1 Google User Data

Xentovia uses Google APIs in two places. Sign in with Google requests your basic profile (name, email address and Google account identifier) and uses it only to match you to an existing Xentovia account and sign you in. Google Calendar connect, which a business chooses to turn on, requests access to view the calendars on the account, read free/busy times and create, change and cancel events. We use it only to offer open appointment slots to the business's customers and to add, update or cancel the bookings they make in the calendar the business selected.

We store the OAuth token for a connected calendar encrypted in our secret store, and we do not keep copies of calendar events beyond the bookings our service created. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models. People at Xentovia do not read it except with your permission, for security purposes, to comply with law, or where the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

Xentovia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect a calendar at any time from the integrations settings in the Xentovia console, which deletes the stored token. You can also remove Xentovia's access from your Google Account permissions page. To have any remaining Google user data deleted, email hello@xentovia.ai.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on our website and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of our Services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
