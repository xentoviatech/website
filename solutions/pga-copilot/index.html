PGA filings got harder. Here's why.

A 36-second look at the three rules that tightened US PGA filings — and how a copilot built below the H350722 line fits in.

Why now

Three rules tightened. One quarter.

None of them were about AI. All three changed how a formal entry has to look in 2026 — and the math on PGA filings.

CBP HQ H350722 · Jan 2026

The line was drawn on AI in customs

Unlicensed AI may classify HS only to the 6-digit international level. An unlicensed entity cannot decide what data appears on an entry. Most tools predate the ruling.

0

End of §321 de minimis

Formal entry volume exploded

~4.2B packages/year that came in duty-free now need formal entries. Many of them flag PGAs they didn't a year ago.

0

Lacey PPQ-505 paper cutoff · Jan 1, 2026

Paper PPQ-505 stopped being accepted

Filers must use ACE or APHIS LAWGS. Lacey Phase VII expanded scope at the same time — furniture, essential oils, sporting goods, housewares.

0

What it does

Three agencies in v1. The ones whose rules just changed.

Every field comes with a citation. Every field comes with a confidence score. Every field is yours to override.

FDA Prior Notice · food

Filed via ABI/ACE/ITDS or FDA PNSI — pre-filled, you approve.

Input

  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Manufacturer registration

Output

  • Filled Prior Notice fields (JSON)
  • Citation per field (e.g., 21 CFR 1.281)
  • Confidence chip per field

EPA TSCA §13 · chemicals/polymers

Positive or negative TSCA §13 statement — with the docs to back it.

Input

  • Commercial invoice
  • SDS / MSDS
  • Certificate of analysis

Output

  • TSCA §13 import certification (positive/negative)
  • Citation to 15 USC §2612 + relevant TSCA §§5/6/7
  • Confidence chip per field

Lacey PPQ-505 · wood / plant products

Species, genus, country of harvest — pre-filled, citation per claim.

Input

  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Product spec sheet

Output

  • PPQ-505 declaration (ACE / LAWGS)
  • Citation to 16 USC §3372 + Phase VII scope
  • Confidence chip per field
