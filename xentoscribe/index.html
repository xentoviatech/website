THE PROBLEM

Report writing is 60% of a pathologist's day.

15–30 minutes per report

Manual typing into templates wastes diagnostic time. Every minute spent formatting a document is a minute away from the microscope.

Dictation tools don't understand pathology

Generic speech-to-text can't handle IHC markers, synoptic fields, or complex medical terminology, leaving you to fix errors manually.

Reports stuck in Word docs

Unstructured text documents offer no data insights, no easy analytics on turnaround times, and no FHIR compliance for the future.

Slide photos live on WhatsApp

There is no seamless connection between the microscope images you capture on your phone and the final patient case file on your desktop.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps. Under three minutes.

01 · DICTATE

Open a case, tap the mic, describe your findings. Works perfectly in English infused with Hindi medical terms.

02 · AI STRUCTURES

Gemini AI fills every template field — gross, micro, diagnosis, IHC — with accurate field-level provenance instantly.

03 · REVIEW & SIGN

Edit any field, re-dictate individual sections if needed, then digitally sign with a secure SHA-256 hash.

04 · EXPORT

Export as PDF on your letterhead, DOCX, FHIR R4, JSON, or plain text. Easily send via WhatsApp or email.
