Generic voicebots fail when handling complex appointments, prices, or regulatory requirements. XentoVoX brings deterministic reliability to every phone call.

Tool-Only Grounding

XentoVoX is prohibited from recalling pricing or availability from memory. Every slot and fee is verified via real-time connector tools before spoken to the caller.

Inbound & Missed-Call-Text-Back

Instantly answer inbound calls via SIP or WebRTC. If a line is busy, automated SMS text-back triggers an instant AI chat or callback funnel.

TCPA & BAA Compliance

Outbound calls pass an explicit pre-dial consent ledger. Healthcare tenants route through PHI-safe cascaded STT → BAA LLM → TTS pipelines automatically.

Declarative Skill Packs

Configure persona, tool parameters, house notes, and transfer logic in YAML. Deploy specialized agents for clinics, hotels, salons, or auto services in minutes.

Post-Call QA & LLM Judge

Every call transcript is analyzed post-hangup by deterministic metric rules and an LLM judge to score sentiment, ungrounded claims, and resolution rates.
